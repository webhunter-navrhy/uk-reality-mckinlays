Building Plot with Planning Potential for a Four-Bedroom Detached Chalet Bungalow

A residential building plot with provision for a spacious 1½-storey, four-bedroom detached chalet bungalow, subject to the relevant planning conditions and restrictions.

The proposed accommodation has been designed with flexible family living in mind, combining generous ground-floor space with a first floor that can be closed off when not required, making it particularly suited to those looking for a home that can adapt over time.

Proposed Masterplan

The proposed property comprises a 1½-storey, four-bedroom detached chalet bungalow, with a front-facing gable and feature bay windows adding architectural interest to the street scene.

The design provides:

•Three double bedrooms and one single bedroom

•Spacious lounge with feature bay window

•Open-plan kitchen/dining area with a walk-in glazed bay and French doors opening onto the garden

•Separate utility room

•Family bathroom

•Separate shower room

•Storage off the entrance hall and landing

•Feature dormer window to the fourth bedroom

•Parking and/or garage provision in accordance with the proposed site layout

Key Features

The proposed home benefits from a number of attractive design features, including a walk-in glazed bay to the kitchen/dining area, creating a bright and sociable space with direct access to the garden.

The lounge features a further bay window, providing an attractive focal point and enhancing the sense of space.

Bedroom three is particularly generous and benefits from roof lights, while the fourth bedroom incorporates a feature dormer window.

The first-floor accommodation can be closed off when not required, providing useful flexibility for changing lifestyles and making the design particularly appealing to those looking for a long-term, adaptable home.
