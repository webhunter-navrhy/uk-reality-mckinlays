Description

A charming period home offering generous accommodation across two floors, retaining attractive original features including tiled flooring and decorative ceiling roses. Set behind a front garden with steps to the entrance, the property provides two reception rooms, a spacious kitchen/breakfast room and three well‑proportioned bedrooms, making it an ideal home for those seeking character and practicality.

The ground floor comprises a welcoming entrance hall, a bay‑fronted lounge with period fireplace (display only) and an adjoining dining room with glazed doors opening to the garden. The kitchen/breakfast room extends to over 17ft and includes a range of units, built‑in oven and hob, tiled flooring and useful understairs storage. Upstairs, the landing offers loft access and storage, leading to three bedrooms—two doubles and a generous single—along with a family bathroom with bath and shower over.

Outside, the rear garden is enclosed and provides a path to the rear gate and garage area. A pedestrian door links the garden to the single garage, which includes power, light and an obscure window. An external store houses the boiler and there is also an outside tap. The property offers a blend of period charm and practical space, with the added benefit of a garage and rear access.

Yeovil offers a wide range of excellent shopping, business and cultural facilities; there are supermarkets, a shopping centre, leisure centre and schools from primary through to secondary and the Yeovil College within easy reach. Regular bus services run within the town also serving nearby villages and neighbouring towns. Mainline railway stations will be found at Yeovil Junction as well as Pen Mill, good road links to the A30, A303 and A37 are all close by. The South Coast is approximately 25 miles away.
